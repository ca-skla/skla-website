# SKLA Insights — Article Briefs

*Companion to `CONTENT-STRATEGY.md`. One brief per article in the priority queue.
Each brief is everything a writer (or partner) needs to produce a publish-ready page using
`insights-article-template.html`. Briefs are deliberately structural — the **facts, figures,
form names and dates must be partner-verified at the time of writing**, because tax rules
change and 2026 introduced several renames (Form 13 → 128; 15CA/15CB → 145/146).*

---

## How to use these briefs

1. Pick a brief. Copy `insights-article-template.html` → its slug (given in the brief).
2. Replace the head tags, schema, breadcrumb, H1 and body using the brief's outline.
3. Write from real mandate experience where you can — that's the ranking edge no competitor has.
4. Verify every number, rate, threshold, form name and deadline against current law.
5. Keep the FAQ block and the FAQPage schema identical.
6. Add the new URL to `sitemap.xml`; flip its card on `insights.html` from "Coming soon" to a link.
7. Internal-link once, naturally, to the matching service section.

**House style:** short sentences, plain English, premium-advisory voice. One H1. Descriptive
anchor text, never "click here". Review byline on every article (E-E-A-T).

**Audience tag colours (already in CSS):** `t-nri`, `t-investor`, `t-business`, `t-newact`.
